09:45 ARRIVAL · ALLOW A FLEXIBLE MORNING

Changi Terminal 2 → Village Hotel Albert Court

ONE TRAIN CHANGE

Preferred with luggage: Changi Airport CG2 → Expo CG1 / DT35 → Rochor DT13 → hotel. This prioritises fewer changes, not the absolute fastest journey.

CG2 Changi Airport→ 1 stopCG1 Expochange to blue lineDT35 Expo → DT13 Rochor→ Exit A / short walk
  1. Land at 9:45am; clear immigration, collect bags and pass customs. Allow roughly 60–90 minutes, possibly longer. Aim to start the train journey around 11–11:30am; these are planning allowances, not guaranteed queues. Skip a Jewel detour today.
  2. Follow “Train to City / MRT” signs in Terminal 2 to Changi Airport station (CG2). It is connected to T2/T3; no terminal shuttle is needed for your booked T2 arrival. Use lifts and wide fare gates with bags or a stroller. Allow time at the ticket office before tapping in.
  3. At CG2, take the airport-branch train towards Tanah Merah; get off at Expo, the first stop. Do not continue to Tanah Merah if following this one-change option.
  4. At Expo, follow the blue Downtown Line signs (DT35). Change within the interchange, following the signs rather than a street-exit route. Board towards Bukit Panjang. Allow approximately 5–10 minutes for the change with luggage.
  5. Stay on the blue line until Rochor (DT13). This is a long ride through the city loop, so passing Bayfront and Bugis before Rochor is normal. Do not get off at Downtown just because you are heading to the city. Follow station displays if service patterns change.
  6. Leave Rochor via Exit A and walk to 180 Albert Street. The hotel quotes about 200m / 3 minutes; allow 5–10 with luggage and crossings. Follow the linked walking route. For step-free access, follow lift signs or ask staff; the lift exit may differ from Exit A.

How to pay for MRT

Each adult needs their own accepted contactless Visa/Mastercard or travel card. Enable overseas use. Tap in and out with the same physical card or same phone/watch; do not switch between a card and its mobile-wallet version.

Foreign Visa/Mastercard transit payments carry a S$0.60 fee per card per day of use, plus fares and any bank FX fees. No SimplyGo app is needed just to tap and travel. Alternatively ask the ticket office about a stored-value travel card.

Budget roughly S$3 per adult for the airport ride and S$2 per adult for each short city ride as planning allowances—not quoted fares. Two adults making the planned three rides: allow around S$15–18 including foreign-card daily fees, excluding bank charges.

Your child’s free-travel card

For a child under 7, not attending primary school, and 0.9m or taller: get a free Child Concession Card at the SimplyGo Ticket Office before the first ride. Bring the child’s original passport and the applying adult’s original passport.

Children under 0.9m can travel free with a fare-paying adult without this card. Do not buy an adult tourist pass for your child without checking eligibility first.

Changi Airport MRT ticket office lists 8am–9pm daily. If you cannot find it, ask station staff. The birthday during this trip does not change under-7 eligibility.

A FIRST EVENING AROUND THE BAY

Skyline, sand & a little spectacle.

FREE ATTRACTIONS

No attraction ticket is needed for Merlion Park, PropNex Family Zone or Spectra. Allow roughly 4–5km total evening walking, spread over several hours—this is a planning estimate. Bring a stroller if you have one, water and rain protection. Do not add paid domes or Garden Rhapsody tonight.

4:15PM

Hotel → Rochor → Downtown

Walk back to Rochor. Take the blue Downtown Line towards Expo: Rochor DT13 → Bugis DT14 → Promenade DT15 → Bayfront DT16 → Downtown DT17. Four stops, no train change. Allow 30–40 minutes overall to reach Merlion, including station access and the final walk.

At Downtown, Exit A serves the OUE Bayfront / One Marina Boulevard side. Walk towards the waterfront and One Fullerton, then Merlion Park (allow 15–20 minutes). The street-level lift is at Exit E; use it for a stroller and allow extra walking. Raffles Place is nearer Merlion, but Downtown avoids another train change.

Indian dinner before playtime

Indian Express at Rasapura Masters, The Shoppes B2-50, is the practical food-court choice. Allow about 40–45 minutes, longer if Saturday queues build. MBS lists the food court’s general hours as 10am–11pm; individual stall availability can vary.

Choose available chicken, seafood or vegetarian dishes and request mild food for your child. Confirm no beef or pork in the chosen dish, stock or sauce. Halal certification does not mean beef-free.
